Privacy Policy for Valley Rail: Train Tycoon

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Short version: this game does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. It has no account system and no server. Everything it saves stays on your own device.

Valley Rail: Train Tycoon ("we", "us") is a free offline train and city building game. This policy explains what the game does and does not do with your data. It applies to the Android app with the package name com.valleyrail.tycoon.

Information We Collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifier. There is no sign-in, no account, and no user profile stored on any server, because there is no server. The game has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

Data Stored On Your Device

To let you continue a game, a small amount of data is saved locally on your device using Android's standard app storage. This data never leaves your device:

  • Saved games: your autosave and manual save, holding the map, your railways, stations, trains, towns and money.
  • Preferences: settings such as sound, music and tutorial hints.

Internet Access

The game is played entirely offline. It declares Android's internet permission because the Unity engine adds it to every build by default, but the game itself never opens a network connection: it has no online features, no leaderboards, and nothing to upload.

Third Parties

We do not share, sell, rent, or transmit any data to third parties, because no data is collected in the first place. The game contains no analytics libraries, no advertising SDKs, no crash-reporting services, and no social media integrations.

Advertising and Purchases

The game contains no advertisements and no in-app purchases. The money in the game is fictional, is only ever earned by running your railway, and cannot be bought, sold or exchanged for anything of value.

Children's Privacy

Valley Rail is a building game suitable for all ages, with no violence, gambling or chat. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, none is knowingly collected from children.

Your Control Over Your Data

Since all data is stored locally, you can remove it at any time:

  • Go to Android Settings → Apps → Valley Rail → Storage → Clear data, or
  • Uninstall the game, which deletes all of its local data from your device.

There is no server-side data for us to delete, and no deletion request is necessary.

Changes To This Policy

If a future version of the game changes what it collects — for example, if online leaderboards are added — this page will be updated and the date above will change.
