Privacy Policy for Valley Rail: Train Tycoon

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Short version: this app shows ads from Google AdMob. Depending on your consent choices, AdMob may use device identifiers to personalise ads. The game has no account system and no server of its own, and your saved games stay on your own device.

Valley Rail: Train Tycoon ("we", "us") is a free, ad-supported train and city building game. This policy explains what the game does and does not do with your data. It applies to the Android app with the package name com.valleyrail.tycoon.

Information We Collect

We collect nothing ourselves. The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifier. There is no sign-in, no account, and no user profile stored on any server, because we run no server. The game has no analytics and no crash reporting. Its only third-party SDK is Google AdMob, which shows the ads and is described under Advertising below.

Data Stored On Your Device

To let you continue a game, a small amount of data is saved locally on your device using Android's standard app storage. This data never leaves your device:

  • Saved games: your autosave and manual save, holding the map, your railways, stations, trains, towns and money.
  • Preferences: settings such as sound, music and tutorial hints.

Internet Access

The game itself is played offline: it has no online features, no leaderboards, and nothing to upload. It uses the internet only so Google AdMob can ask for your consent and load ads; the game still works without a connection, just without ads.

Third Parties

We do not share, sell, rent, or transmit any data to third parties ourselves. The one third-party service in the game is Google AdMob, which receives device and ad-interaction data directly as described in Google's partner-sites policy. The game contains no analytics libraries, no crash-reporting services, and no social media integrations.

Advertising and Purchases

This app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the title and play screens and an occasional full-screen ad when you return to the title screen. Depending on your consent choices, AdMob may use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) to personalise ads. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent on first launch, and you can reset the advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device's Google settings. There are no in-app purchases. The money in the game is fictional, is only ever earned by running your railway, and cannot be bought, sold or exchanged for anything of value.

Children's Privacy

Valley Rail is a building game suitable for all ages, with no violence, gambling or chat. We collect no personal information from anyone, so none is knowingly collected from children. The game is not directed at children, and its ads come from Google AdMob.

Your Control Over Your Data

Since all data is stored locally, you can remove it at any time:

  • Go to Android Settings → Apps → Valley Rail → Storage → Clear data, or
  • Uninstall the game, which deletes all of its local data from your device.

We hold no server-side data to delete. Data used by Google AdMob is governed by Google's privacy policy and your Google account and device settings.

Changes To This Policy

If a future version of the game changes what it collects — for example, if online leaderboards are added or the ad setup changes — this page will be updated and the date above will change.
